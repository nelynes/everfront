Label Maker

Real labels for every cart on your shelf. Open your EverDrive's SD card, check what each game gets, and write the labels straight back. Nothing is uploaded: your card stays on your computer.

  1. 1 Open your SD card

    Put the EverDrive's SD card in your computer, then choose the card itself (the drive, not a folder on it).

    Your browser can read the card but not write to it, so your labels will download as a zip to copy over. Chrome or Edge can write them to the card for you.

  2. 2 Check the labels

    Each game gets the best label found: your own scans first, then labels already on your card, then the slotPlus and Slot art sets, then the game's logo on a colour from its box art. On any cart, ↻ tries the next label, ＋ uses an image of your own, and ✕ leaves a plain label.

    A folder of label images named like your roms.

  3. 3 Write them to the card

    Open your card to start.

Label scans and logos belong to each game's publisher; they're here so you can label carts of games you own. Labels come from the slotPlus art set (including scans from ScreenScraper, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), Slot's art set, and libretro-thumbnails. Games are identified with libretro-database (CC BY-SA 4.0). Logo labels follow Cart Studio+.
